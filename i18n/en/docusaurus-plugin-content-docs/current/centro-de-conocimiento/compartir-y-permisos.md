# Sharing and permissions

Folders are shared with people, with [workspaces](/modulos/espacios-de-trabajo) and with agents. You share folders, not individual files: to give access to a file, share the folder that contains it.

## Share a folder {#compartir-una-carpeta}

1. In the folder's menu, click **Share**.
2. In **Add people, workspaces or agents**, search for and choose the recipients. They appear grouped under **People**, **Workspaces** and **Agents**, and are collected under **To add**.
3. Choose each recipient's access level.
4. Click **Save changes**. Nothing is applied until then.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/centro-de-conocimiento/compartir.png" alt="Share window for a folder in the ConsulPoint Knowledge Hub" caption="Share a folder" maxWidth={640} />

A folder can be shared with up to 10 people and 5 agents. To share with a workspace you must be a member of that workspace.

## Access levels {#niveles-de-acceso}

| Level | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Can view** | Open and download the content |
| **Can edit** | Also upload files, create subfolders, rename, move and prepare files for agents |
| **Can manage** | Also share, delete, manage apps and extract data |
| **Read only** | The fixed level for agents: they consult the content without changing it |

The folder access level adds to the person's role: to edit, they also need a role with edit permission on Knowledge Hub. See [Roles](/organizacion/roles).

## Inheritance {#herencia}

Sharing a folder gives access to everything it contains, including its subfolders and anything added later. In a subfolder's **Share** window, that access appears as **Inherited**, with the folder it comes from (**From the "…" folder**), and it can only be changed from that folder.

## Remove access {#quitar-el-acceso}

In the **Share** window, click **Remove access** next to the recipient and then **Save changes**. Until you save, **Undo** reverts it.

When you remove an agent's access, it stops searching that folder.

## Where shared content appears {#dónde-se-ve-lo-compartido}

- Whoever receives a folder finds it in **Shared with me**.
- Members of a workspace find the folders shared with the workspace when working inside it.
- In **My documents**, the **Shared with** column and **View details** (**Who has access**) show who each folder is shared with.
