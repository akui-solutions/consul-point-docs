# Your account

<PageMeta route="/profile" />

Each person manages their account from the **user menu**: the name and photo in the top-right corner. No permission is needed.

## User menu {#menú-de-usuario}

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Email | The account's email, at the top of the menu |
| **Account Settings** | Opens your personal details and password. See [below](#configuración-de-cuenta) |
| **Change appearance** | Turns dark mode on or off |
| **Change language** | **Spanish** or **English** |
| **Log out** | Leaves the platform |

Theme and language are saved in the browser: on another computer or browser you have to choose them again.

### Personal Information {#información-personal}

**Edit information** lets you change:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Photo | JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 500 KB. **Upload photo** or drag it in; **Remove photo** removes it and **Undo** brings back the previous one before saving |
| **Name** and **Last name** | Between 2 and 50 characters |
| **Email** | Cannot be changed |
| **Mobile** | Contact number |

Finish with **Save changes**. The name and photo are what other people in the organization see, for example in shared conversations.

### Password {#contraseña}

**Request password change** sends a link to the account's email to choose a new password, the same as [Reset the password](/introduccion/acceder-a-la-plataforma#recuperar-la-contraseña). The password is not changed inside the platform.

## Log out {#cerrar-sesión}

**Log out**, at the bottom of the menu, leaves the platform in that browser and stops [browser notifications](/introduccion/notificaciones#notificaciones-del-navegador) there. Other sessions open on other devices stay active.

:::tip Shared computers
On a computer used by several people, it is best to log out when you finish.
:::
