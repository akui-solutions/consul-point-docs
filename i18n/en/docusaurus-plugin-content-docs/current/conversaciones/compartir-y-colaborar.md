# Sharing and collaborating

Tools for working with other people or with several conversations at once.

## Sharing a conversation {#compartir-una-conversación}

Everyone with access to a shared conversation can see it and write in it, with the same agent.

1. In the conversation header, click **Share conversation**. It only appears once the conversation has messages.
2. Search for people by first or last name.
3. Click **Save changes**.

- You can share it with up to 9 people besides the person who created it, who appears as **Owner**.
- Only people with access to the conversation's agent are offered.
- **Remove access** removes a person. Anyone who is not the owner can leave with **Leave conversation**, under **More options**.
- While someone else is writing, the message box says **Someone else is typing** and waits for them to finish.

In the list, shared conversations show a mark on the avatar, and the header shows the participants' avatars.

## Second conversation {#segunda-conversación}

Opens another conversation next to the current one, for example to compare how two agents answer the same question.

1. In the header, click **Open side panel**.
2. Choose **Second Conversation**.
3. Start a **New conversation** or continue an existing one.

Drag the divider between the two to resize them. A message can be sent from one conversation to the other. **Close second conversation** removes it from the panel.

## Apps in the side panel {#aplicaciones-en-el-panel-auxiliar}

In **Open side panel**, the **Apps** section lists the extension-type [integrated apps](/aplicaciones-integradas). An app opens in the panel, next to the conversation, and can use its information.

## Conversation details {#detalles-de-la-conversación}

Clicking the conversation title opens its details:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Agent Information** | Its specialists, features and connectors |
| **Conversation Information** | Type, channel, participants, who created it and when |
| **Contact Information** | In channel conversations: the number or identifier and the linked contact |
| **Files shared in this conversation** | The attached files, to download them |

## Deleting a conversation {#eliminar-una-conversación}

In the conversation's menu, in the list, **Delete**. The conversation disappears for all its participants.

:::danger No going back
A deleted conversation cannot be recovered from the platform.
:::

Conversations cannot be renamed.
