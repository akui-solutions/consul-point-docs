# Contacts

<PageMeta route="/contacts">the relevant one on **Contacts**</PageMeta>

**Contacts** brings together the people and entities (companies or organizations) your organization deals with: customers, suppliers, partners… Each contact has a record with their details, their messaging channels and the conversations they have had.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/contactos/contactos.png" alt="List of people in ConsulPoint Contacts" caption="Contacts → People" />

## People and entities {#personas-y-entidades}

The module has two tabs:

| Tab | Content | Filters |
|---|---|---|
| **People** | Name, email, phone, entity, contact types, tags and owner. People who are also platform users carry the **User** badge | Search by name, email or phone; **Contact type**, **Entity**, **Owner**, **Card type** (**Users** or **Contacts**) and **Tag** |
| **Entities** | Legal name, tax ID, number of members, contact types, tags and owner | Search by legal name, trade name or tax ID; **Contact type**, **Owner** and **Tag** |

Contact types and tags are managed in Settings. See [Contact types](/organizacion/tipos-de-contacto) and [Tags](/organizacion/etiquetas).

## Creating a contact {#crear-un-contacto}

**Create** offers three options:

- **New contact**: a person. A first name or last name is enough; you can also add an ID document, entities, contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram…), addresses, contact types, tags, owner and notes.
- **From a user**: creates the record from a platform user.
- **New entity**: a company or organization, to which people are then linked.

When you create a contact, if it looks like an existing one, the platform warns you with **This may already exist**.

## The record {#la-ficha}

When you open a contact you see two tabs:

- **Details**: ID document, owner, contact types and tags; contact details; messaging channels; entities they belong to; addresses and notes. Each block is edited separately.
- **Activity**: the contact's conversations, with their channel (**Internal**, **WhatsApp**, **Telegram** or **External app**) and their status (**Pending**, **In progress** or **Resolved**), and the teams handling them.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/contactos/ficha.png" alt="A contact's record" caption="A contact's record → Details" />

An entity's record shows its **Details** and its **Members**. When you delete an entity, its people remain.

## Contacts and conversations {#contactos-y-conversaciones}

When a conversation arrives through WhatsApp, Telegram or an external app, the platform tries to recognize the contact. If it finds a similar one, the conversation shows **Is this …?** with **Yes, that is them** or **Not now**. You can also link it from the conversation header with **Assign**. See [Channels and human support](/conversaciones/canales-y-atencion-humana#contactos).

## Limits {#límites}

The plan sets the maximum number of people and entities. See [Credits and limits](/organizacion/creditos-y-limites#límites-del-plan).
