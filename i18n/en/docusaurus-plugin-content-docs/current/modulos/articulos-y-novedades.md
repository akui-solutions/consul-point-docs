# Articles & Updates

<PageMeta route="/resources">the relevant one on **Articles & Updates**</PageMeta>

A space to publish announcements, updates and articles for the whole organization: a new policy, the training calendar, a team announcement. The most recent articles also appear in the [Home](/modulos/inicio) carousel.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/articulos-y-novedades/articulos.png" alt="List of articles in ConsulPoint" caption="Articles & Updates" />

## Who can do what {#quién-puede-hacer-qué}

With the default roles:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Read, publish, edit and delete their articles |
| User | Read, publish and edit their articles. Not delete |
| Collaborator | Read only |

An article can only be edited or deleted by the people who wrote it.

## Reading articles {#leer-los-artículos}

The list shows the most recent article first, in large format, and then the rest in cards with their image, title, description and date. **Search by name...** filters by title within the page you are viewing.

When you open an article you see the main image, the author, the date (**Published on:**) and the full content.

## Publishing an article {#publicar-un-artículo}

1. Click **Create new entry**.
2. Fill in the fields:

   | Field | Description |
   |---|---|
   | **Title** | Required, up to 100 characters. It cannot repeat another article's title |
   | **Description** | Optional, up to 80 characters. It is the summary shown in the cards and in the Home carousel |
   | **Main Post Banner** | Optional. JPG, PNG or GIF image, up to 5 MB. It is shown in the card and at the top of the article |
   | **Content** | Required, up to 30,000 characters |

3. Click **Create post**. The article is published right away and everyone with access can see it.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/articulos-y-novedades/crear-articulo.png" alt="Form to create an article in ConsulPoint" caption="Create an article" />

:::info Published instantly
There are no drafts or scheduling: creating the article publishes it. Publishing it does not send notices to anyone either; if it matters, it is best to announce it through another channel.
:::

### The content editor {#el-editor-de-contenido}

The content supports headings, bold, italics, underline, strikethrough, lists, quotes, code, links, paragraph alignment and images. Images inserted in the text can be up to 1 MB each.

## Editing and deleting {#editar-y-eliminar}

From the open article:

- **Edit** opens the form with the current content. **Update Post** saves the changes. The main image can be changed, removed or, before saving, reverted to the original.
- **Delete** removes the article.

:::danger Permanent deletion
A deleted article cannot be recovered.
:::

## On Home {#en-inicio}

The **Featured Article** carousel on [Home](/modulos/inicio) shows the 3 most recent articles, with their image, title and description. Clicking one opens it in full.

## In workspaces {#en-los-espacios-de-trabajo}

Each [workspace](/modulos/espacios-de-trabajo) has its own articles: the organization's articles do not appear inside a workspace, nor the other way round. People who belong only to a workspace, without being part of the organization, can read its articles but not publish them.

## Good practices {#buenas-prácticas}

:::tip Articles that get read
- A clear title and a description that says in one sentence what it is about: that is what shows on Home.
- A landscape main image that still reads well when cropped in the card.
- For documentation the agents need to consult, use the [Knowledge Hub](/modulos/centro-de-conocimiento) instead: agents do not read articles.
:::
