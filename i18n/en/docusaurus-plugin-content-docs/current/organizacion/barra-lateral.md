# Sidebar

<PageMeta route="/settings/preferences">permission on **Pinned items** and **Apps**</PageMeta>

In **Settings → Sidebar** you choose the [integrated apps](/aplicaciones-integradas) that appear in the **Pinned** section of the sidebar, so they open with one click.

<Screenshot src="/img/en/configuracion/barra-lateral.png" alt="Sidebar settings" caption="Settings → Sidebar" />

## Pin an app {#fijar-una-aplicación}

1. Click **Pin item**.
2. Search for the app and click the button to pin it.
3. Administrators also choose who sees it:
   - **Only for me**: only in their own sidebar (this is the default, and the only option for non-administrators);
   - **For everyone**: in the sidebar of the whole organization.

In the list, apps pinned for everyone carry the **Global** label. Each person sees the apps pinned for everyone plus their own, **up to 10** in total.

## Reorder and remove {#ordenar-y-quitar}

- You reorder them by **dragging** them, here or in the sidebar itself.
- **Remove from the sidebar** takes them off. Only an administrator can remove the ones pinned for everyone.

The gear icon in the **Pinned** section of the sidebar opens this same screen. See [Navigation](/introduccion/navegacion).
